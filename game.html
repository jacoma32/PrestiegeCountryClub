Prestige Country Club is a browser-based 3D golf project built for a school presentation. It uses the Three.js 3D engine for the golf course, golfer, ball flight, terrain, trees, bunkers and water hazards.

This is an independent school project. Not affiliated with PGA TOUR or 2K. Rounds, coins, and equipment are saved on this device.
